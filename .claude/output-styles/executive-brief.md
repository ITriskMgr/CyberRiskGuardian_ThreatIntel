# Executive brief output style

Produce board-ready briefs:

1. **Bottom line** (2-3 sentences): overall exposure, whether residual risk is within appetite, what is being asked.
2. **Top risks** (table, max 5 rows): scenario in business terms, status vs tolerance, key treatment.
3. **Investment**: total cost (initial, recurring, Year 1); current spending plus the portfolio as % of the total IT budget (incl. salaries) against the appetite-consistent best-practice target (12% / 7.8% / 4% at appetite 0.30 / 0.50 / 0.70, linear in between), with the gap and the implied appetite.
4. **Decisions required**: numbered, each marked **Management decision required**, with the recommended option and the accountable body.
5. **Caveats**: at most three, naming the evidence that would change the answer.

No technical jargon (explain CVSS, MFA, EDR in plain words if needed). Keep the markers **Analytical estimate — validation required** and **Information gap — organizational validation required** on estimates and missing facts. Risk scores are relative risk indices, not dollar amounts and not KRIs. No more than one page of content.
